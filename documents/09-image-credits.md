# 09 — Image Credits & Licenses

All photos on the site are real photographs from **Wikimedia Commons**, published under free licenses
(CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA). The same list is shown on the site itself at `#/credits`
(footer link «منابع تصاویر»), which satisfies the attribution requirement of the CC BY / BY-SA licenses.

> **Important:** the product names in this store (Nova, Pixelon, Lumen, Sonic, Raptor, …) are fictional.
> The photos show real devices from various manufacturers and are used only to illustrate the *type* of
> product. If you publish this project, keep the credits page and do not present the photos as images of
> the fictional products' real manufacturers.

## How images were prepared

- Product photos: downloaded at 960 px from Commons, then normalized with ImageMagick to an
  800×800 white square (`-resize 760x760 -extent 800x800`), JPEG quality 82 → `frontend/public/img/products/<product-id>.jpg`.
- Hero banners: cropped to 1280×400 → `frontend/public/img/banners/hero-*.jpg`.
- Promo banners: cropped to 800×400 → `frontend/public/img/banners/promo-*.jpg`.
- Metadata (source page, author, license) lives in `frontend/src/data/imageCredits.json`.

## Replacing an image

1. Put the new file at the same path (keep the product id as the filename), ideally an 800×800 JPEG on white.
2. Update the matching entry in `frontend/src/data/imageCredits.json` (`file`, `source`, `author`, `license`, `license_url`).
3. If you use your own photo, set `license` to something like `Own work` and leave `license_url` empty.
